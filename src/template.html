THE RULES OF THE ROAD

Race. Roast. Repeat.

Your scooter accelerates automatically and reaches cruising speed quickly. Push the left stick diagonally up to boost while steering. On phones, one short Greek subtitle replaces the large rider bubbles; turn Dialogue text off in Settings if you want a clear screen. Dodge the city obstacles, take ramps and collect green repairs or blue energy. A scooter collision damages both riders, triggers automatic return fire, and makes both characters insult each other.
