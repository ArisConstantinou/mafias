MORE RULES & GAME MODES

THREE FIGHT BUTTONS

Move with the left stick or WASD. Punch, Kick and Dodge stay on screen. Three successful hits on the same rival unlock a temporary Heavy Punch or Heavy Kick, based on the majority of hits. Your fighter faces a nearby rival automatically.

DIZZY / GRAB / SPIN

Hits and blocks drain stamina. At zero stamina a rival sways with circling stars. Move close and tap Grab over them. Then choose Slam or Spin over the grabbed rival. If you are grabbed, tap Escape over your fighter repeatedly.

PROPS & BLOCK

Move beside a prop and tap Pick Up over it. While holding it, tap Throw near your fighter; the throw aims at a nearby rival. Stop moving briefly to block automatically. Blocking reduces damage but consumes stamina. Dodge moves in your chosen direction and gives brief protection.

THE CHAOS CLOUD

Three or more nearby fighters and recent attacks start a brawl cloud. Their HP, attack timing, injuries and positions remain real. Heads pop out after impacts. Move or dodge out.

INJURIES MATTER

Below 75 HP: bruised. Below 50: beat up. Below 25: wrecked. Repeated head hits leave black eyes. Leg damage causes a visible limp and slows movement. At zero HP, you're out.

THREE WAYS TO WIN

Last One: eliminate all three rivals. Score Brawl: most points in 90 seconds, with respawns. The Crown: hold it for 45 accumulated seconds; knockdowns drop it. Incoming damage fills Rage for your next powered-up attack.

Fictional, non-graphic slapstick. One local human player plus three AI opponents. No online multiplayer or split-screen in this build.
